# Code of conduct

We are committed to a technically rigorous, welcoming project.

Participants must be respectful, assume good faith, critique ideas rather than
people, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct,
doxxing, or sustained disruption. Technical disagreement is expected; personal
attacks are not.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
necessary to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately through
the organization contact methods or to repository administrators. Reports will
be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, review threads,
community spaces, and public representation of the project.
